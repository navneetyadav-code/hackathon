What is Thikana?

Your bridge to affordable coliving.

Urban living is expensive. Thikana is a platform designed to solve the modern housing crisis by connecting people who want to share spaces. Whether you have an empty room to fill or you're looking to split rent in a new city, we make the process safe, transparent, and easy.

Community First

Find roommates based on lifestyle habits, interests, and budget, not just location.

Verified Affordability

Listings are vetted to ensure you get transparent pricing with no hidden broker fees.

Safe & Secure

In-app messaging and verified user profiles keep your search secure and private.
